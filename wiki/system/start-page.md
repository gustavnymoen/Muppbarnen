# Start page and menu

## Parts

| Part | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Header | `app/components/SiteHeader.tsx` | Only the menu button, without a border (TECH-006: the site's name is no longer shown as a heading). It is inside `Page`, outside the scrolling `PageContent`, so it stays in place. `<Page home>` keeps the name as a visually hidden `h1`, so the page has a heading for screen readers; other pages have their own `h1`. |
| Menu | `app/components/SiteMenu.tsx` | A hamburger on every screen size. The button "Meny" has `aria-expanded`; the list is a `nav` with "Start" and "Admin", both links; "Admin" (to `/admin`, [Admin page](admin.md)) is last and in the muted text colour, and nothing else on the page points to it. |
| Deck | `app/components/BirthdayDeck.tsx`, `lib/deck.ts` | The birthday deck (MUPP-004): one card per date with kids' birthdays plus Christmas Eve, nearest first. `lib/deck.ts` builds the cards on the server and sends only the card's day and month, each kid's age and the order, never the birthdate or the birth year (rule in [Rules](../../specs/rules.md), [MUPP-020](../../specs/MUPP-020-birthday-day-on-cards/solution.md)). |
| Wish list | `app/components/WishList.tsx`, `app/components/transition.ts`, `lib/wishes.ts`, `app/actions.ts` | The "Önskelista" button on a kid's card; the list unfolds to the wishes (rows, see below), which scroll in an inner box (a group named "Önskningar", focusable for keyboard scrolling) inside the card, and the button is swapped for a 32 px round `compact` `IconButton` centred on the bottom edge line of the list box, half outside it, with a 44 px target (a `before:` pseudo-element; [MUPP-028](../../specs/MUPP-028-close-button-on-line/solution.md)): an arrow up ("Fäll ihop önskelistan") on a card with one kid (MUPP-005-R6), a cross ("Stäng önskelistan") on a card with several kids and on Christmas Eve (MUPP-013-R5, MUPP-005-R7, R8). The rows' box ends above the button (`pb-5` on the list box), so the button never covers a row. Opening and closing move ([MUPP-016](../../specs/MUPP-016-wishlist-open-transition/solution.md)): a single-kid list grows out of the "Önskelista" button, moved and scaled (`growScale`, [MUPP-029](../../specs/MUPP-029-single-kid-close-motion/solution.md)); a kid's view on the other cards grows from the pressed button (`growFrom`); browsing to a card with its list open has nothing to grow from and does not move; closing makes a ghost copy that shrinks (scaled whole, [MUPP-022](../../specs/MUPP-022-close-animation-glitch/solution.md)) to the button while the real view is already closed (`makeGhost`, `shrinkTo` in `transition.ts`); with reduced motion nothing moves. A close with a finger or mouse leaves no focus ring on the button that gets the focus (`data-quiet-focus`, removed at the next key press or when the focus leaves). |
| Sizes | `app/components/SizesButton.tsx`, `app/components/Dialog.tsx` | A child's sizes (MUPP-014): the "Storlekar" button on a card with one kid (folded list), an icon button named "Storlekar för {name}" beside the wish button on a card with several kids, and an icon button "Storlekar" in the top right corner of every open kid view (it replaces the text button). It opens a modal dialog "Storlekar för {name}" with a row per saved size (Kläder, Skor, Huvud, Handskar) or "Inga storlekar inlagda än."; the button exists even when no sizes are saved. The sizes come from `listDeckInput` through `buildDeck` (`DeckKid.sizes`, trimmed) and are plain text. `Dialog` is the shared `<dialog>` look; it keeps Esc and key presses inside from reaching the deck, so Esc closes only the dialog. |
| Fit name | `app/components/FitName.tsx` | A kid's name on one line (MUPP-013-R3): shrinks the text one pixel at a time down to 12 px, then cuts it with "…"; the full name stays in the text for screen readers. Used on the two-kid card and the Christmas tiles. |

## How it behaves

- The menu closes with Escape, a choice, a click outside it, or Tab out of it. Escape and a choice return focus to the menu button.
- The deck is a carousel: the selected card is in the middle, with the previous and next seen dimmed at the sides. It is browsed by swiping, the buttons "Föregående kort" and "Nästa kort", or the arrow keys, and wraps around. With reduced motion the cards change without moving.
- Every card is the same size (up to 40 rem high) and centred; the browse buttons sit at the sides of the card. The header is centred, with the day and month ("17 mars") and, on the first card, the mark "✦ Nästa födelsedag ✦" under it.
- A card with one kid shows the picture, name, age and the wish list (folded: one tall "Önskelista" button with an arrow down, in the kid's colour, no number of wishes); a birthday card with several kids shows a miniature per kid, one above the other (a large picture of 104 px, name, age and a 11 rem wish list button without a number), each kid in its own equal part of the card (R1) with a thin line between the kids, 60 % wide in the muted text colour (R2), and the Christmas card shows its kids as tiles, two by two (picture above the name); the button or row opens that kid as an ordinary card with the list unfolded. Christmas Eve holds all kids, without age, on a green night background drawn in SVG behind the content (`ChristmasBackdrop.tsx`: a garland of lights, stars, snow and fir trees).
- **Wish rows (MUPP-005):** each row shows the headline, "Paxad" (orange, `--color-claimed`) if claimed and "Kan köpas flera gånger" if unlimited. The order (`sortWishes`, applied by `buildDeck`) is claimable, then unlimited, then claimed, each group in the admin order (MUPP-019-R2). An unlimited row has an info button (`InfoBubble`, a frameless `IconButton`: the icon alone with a round `edge` tint on hover and press, still 44 × 44 px; [MUPP-027](../../specs/MUPP-027-info-button-no-frame/solution.md)) in the checkbox's place, which shows the explanation as a bubble over the row (Esc, a second press or a press elsewhere hides it; MUPP-019-R1). The headline is a button that unfolds the description, "Storlek: …", the link (only `http:`/`https:`, `target="_blank"`, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`; `webLink`), and for a claimed row "Paxad av {name} {date}" in orange. The description is written "Beskrivning: …" (MUPP-019-R3). A title (up to 60 characters; older ones may be up to 120) wraps inside the row, also a single long word (`min-w-0` on the flex items, `wrap-anywhere`), is cut after two lines with an ellipsis until the row is opened, and is `text-sm` when `isLongTitle` says it is over 24 characters ([MUPP-023](../../specs/MUPP-023-long-wish-titles/solution.md)); the claim dialog wraps it too. All texts are plain text. A row that is neither claimed nor unlimited has a checkbox named after the wish; with at least one marked, "Paxa ({n})" appears centred in a fixed row under the rows, above the fold-up button, so it is seen at once also in a long list ([MUPP-028](../../specs/MUPP-028-close-button-on-line/solution.md)), and opens a modal `<dialog>` (focus in the name field, the marked gifts listed). The marks live in the open list only, so folding it or closing the card clears them. A claimed row that is not unlimited (`canUnclaim`) has an undo arrow instead (`IconButton` `frameless` with `UndoIcon` in `--color-claimed`, named "Ta bort paxningen på {title}"; [MUPP-006](../../specs/MUPP-006-unclaim-gifts/solution.md)); it opens `UnclaimDialog`, an `alertdialog` with the gift, who claimed it, a warning to remove only your own claims, and "Avbryt" (focused first) and "Ta bort", short so both fit side by side at 320 px.
- **Claiming:** the server action `claimWishes` (`app/actions.ts`) calls `hasSiteSession()`, then `guardWrite()`, then `claimWishes` in `lib/family.ts`, which trims and checks the name (1–60 characters) and the ids and claims each wish with `UPDATE … WHERE claimed_by IS NULL AND unlimited = 0`, so a wish that is taken, unlimited or missing is never changed. The visitor is told which could not be claimed, and the page is refreshed (`revalidatePath`). Removing a claim (MUPP-006) is the server action `unclaimWish` in the same order (`hasSiteSession()`, `guardWrite()`), then `unclaimWish` in `lib/family.ts`, which checks the id and runs `UPDATE … SET claimed_by = NULL, claimed_at = NULL WHERE id = ? AND claimed_by IS NOT NULL AND unlimited = 0`. The status line under the list says "Paxningen på {title} är borttagen." or, if someone else was first, "… var redan borttagen. Listan är uppdaterad.", and focus goes to the button that folds the list. Anyone with the site password may remove any claim: there is no sign-in per person, on purpose.
- **Browsing with a list open (MUPP-017):** the buttons and arrow keys stay on. With a list open on a card with one kid they show the previous or next card with its kid's list open (a card with several kids or Christmas Eve is shown as the overview, focus on its first kid's button); on a card with several kids and on Christmas Eve they change to the previous or next kid on the same card, wrapping, with that kid's list open. `BirthdayDeck.go` does it all; marks are forgotten because each kid's list is its own component (`KidFace` is keyed by kid). Swiping is off while a list is open so it does not clash with scrolling the list, and arrow keys inside a dialog (claim, sizes) do not browse. The screen reader hears "Kort n av N: …" or "{name}, önskelistan är öppen". The wish button is swapped for a button on the bottom line of the list (an arrow up on a card with one kid, a cross otherwise); it gets the focus when the list opens, and the wish button gets it back when the list closes. The focus is moved with `preventScroll` (MUPP-021): the deck's grid is `overflow: hidden` and a browser still scrolls it to reveal a focused card that is sliding in, which left the deck shifted sideways after going forward. Escape closes it too. On a card with several kids and on Christmas Eve the open kid view also has "Tillbaka" in the top left corner (MUPP-015, an icon only since MUPP-017: `IconButton` with `ReturnIcon`, first in the tab order, named "Tillbaka till alla barn" or "Tillbaka till Julafton"); it calls the same `close` as the cross and Escape (list closed, marks forgotten, focus on the kid's button). The card with one kid has no Tillbaka.
- **The top of an open kid view (MUPP-030):** in every view with an open list (a single-kid card with its list unfolded, a kid opened on a card with several kids or on Christmas Eve) the list gets the room. `DeckCardView` puts the date heading (`h2`, or "Julafton") on the row of the corner buttons: the card's top padding is `pt-3` like the buttons' `top-3`, and the header is a 44 px high row (`h-11`) with `px-10`, so the date is centred between the buttons and never under them (it does not wrap, and is `text-sm` under 23rem); the card's side padding is `px-4` instead of `px-6`, so a long name and the list get more room. The "Nästa födelsedag" / "Nästa: Julafton" chip is left out, and `KidFace` shows only a 40 px picture with the name (`h3`, still the view's heading) on one line to its right, centred together; the name is a `FitName`, so a long name shrinks to fit instead of wrapping. The age line ("Fyller X år", "Beräknad …") is left out too. The closed cards are unchanged.
- The mark on the next card ("✦ Nästa födelsedag ✦", "Nästa: Julafton") is always yellow (`--color-child-sun`), whatever the kid's colour (MUPP-013-R4).
- A kid who is not born yet (birthdate after today, MUPP-011) has no age: the birthday card shows "Beräknad {dag och månad}", and from the birthdate on the kid is an ordinary one whose next birthday is the first (age at least 1). The Christmas card lists the kid like any other.
- With no kids the start page shows one card, "Inga barn har lagts till än.".
- "Today" is the date in Sweden (`Europe/Stockholm`). A birthday on 29 February is on 28 February in years without one.
- The cards at the sides are `aria-hidden` and `inert`; a live region tells the screen reader which card it is on.
